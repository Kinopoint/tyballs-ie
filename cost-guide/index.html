TY Ball cost guide

What shapes the cost?

Every proposal is built around the actual event: its venue, date, guest estimate and chosen inclusions.

Pricing factors

How pricing comes together

DebsGuru checks current venue and supplier costs against the committee’s priorities, then presents the relevant inclusions and pricing in one proposal.

Venue and date

Location, availability, day of the week and time of year all influence the venue cost.

Guest estimate

A realistic number helps the venue and suppliers calculate the right capacity and per-person cost.

Dinner and service

The menu, service style and dietary arrangements form part of the proposal.

Entertainment

DJ, lighting, photography, photobooth and other additions are shaped around the committee’s priorities.

Transport

Routes, collection points and passenger numbers determine any transport included in the plan.

Event requirements

Staffing, access, timings and venue procedures differ from one location to another.

Send five useful details

The school, county, preferred date or flexibility, realistic guest estimate and the parts of the night that matter most to the committee.
