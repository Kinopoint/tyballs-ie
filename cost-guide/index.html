TY Ball cost guide

Why there is no honest one-price answer.

A useful price is one that reflects the actual venue, date, attendance and experience — not a headline figure that changes after you enquire.

What shapes the proposal

Six variables that make the biggest difference.

DebsGuru does not publish a fixed TY Ball price because venue and supplier costs can change. The committee receives a tailored discussion once the essentials are known.

01

Venue and date

Availability and venue rates vary by location, day of the week and time of year.

02

Attendance

An honest estimate helps venues and suppliers calculate the practical per-person cost.

03

Food and service

The menu, service format and dietary arrangements affect the proposal.

04

Entertainment

DJ, production, photography, photobooths and optional extras are selected around the committee’s priorities.

05

Transport

Transport may depend on routes, collection points and passenger numbers and should be shown clearly when included.

06

Venue-specific requirements

Staffing, access, timing and event requirements can differ between venues.

What to send for an accurate starting conversation

School, county, preferred date or date flexibility, realistic estimated attendance and the parts of the night that matter most to your committee.
