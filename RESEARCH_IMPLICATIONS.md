# TYBalls.ie — implications of the 1 August 2026 market research

Source reviewed: `/Users/kinopoint/Downloads/deep-research-report.md 7`.

The report is strategic research, not a statement of DebsGuru’s operating model. Its recommendations are split below into confirmed launch improvements, claims that require client evidence, and features that belong to a possible future ticketing product.

## Business model boundary

Declan confirmed that TYBalls.ie will launch as a committee-led, tailored enquiry service:

- packages and prices vary by venue and date;
- no fixed minimum or maximum attendance is published;
- the website form starts a conversation and does not reserve a date;
- pricing is confirmed after speaking with the committee;
- full booking terms are issued when the date and proposal are confirmed;
- the project scope does not currently include public events, ticket checkout or customer accounts.

The report also explores a different model: regional multi-school events with public tickets, guest checkout, transfers, refunds, wallet passes and a school portal. These features must not be presented as current DebsGuru services without a separate commercial and operational decision.

## Apply to the launch

1. Position clarity and predictability as the premium benefit, not only spectacle.
2. Explain why prices are tailored before the enquiry form.
3. State repeatedly that an enquiry is not a booking and does not reserve a date.
4. Keep the form mobile-first and ask for an honest attendance estimate.
5. Give committees, parents and schools clear paths to relevant information, while keeping one primary enquiry conversion.
6. Create a dedicated Safety & Planning information area after the client confirms the underlying facts.
7. Publish Privacy, Cookie and Website Terms at launch.
8. Use independent reviews with a visible source when DebsGuru supplies verifiable review links.
9. Keep the hero image as the LCP resource and avoid autoplay video.
10. Measure Core Web Vitals and form conversion without sending personal form data to analytics.
11. Build a content cluster for cost, committee planning, parents, formats and regional intent.
12. Preserve the DebsGuru proof point: personal coordinator and one event per day, subject to final client confirmation for TY Balls.

## Requires written client confirmation

The website must not assert any of the following until DebsGuru confirms the exact operating policy and evidence:

- alcohol policy for under-18 events;
- security staffing and qualifications;
- medical personnel or first-aid cover;
- safeguarding process and responsible persons;
- insurance details;
- transport provision and responsible contacts;
- food, allergy and accessibility processes;
- entry, exit and re-entry rules;
- whether events ever combine multiple schools;
- cancellation, transfer, refund and venue-change rules;
- exact geographic coverage and venue relationships;
- testimonials, ratings, photography or video publication rights.

## Do not publish as current products

The following are research hypotheses, not approved DebsGuru offers:

- Essential at €49;
- Signature at €69;
- Festival at €89;
- any minimum guest count;
- “all inclusive” or “no hidden extras” claims;
- public event listings or remaining-ticket counts;
- direct ticket purchase, Apple Pay, Google Pay or QR tickets;
- free ticket transfers or guaranteed refunds;
- school accounts, ticket dashboards or referral rewards;
- fixed response-time promises;
- named regional venues without a current commercial relationship.

## Revised information architecture

### Phase 1 — agreed website

1. Home.
2. The TY Ball experience.
3. How it works.
4. For committees.
5. For parents and schools.
6. Safety & Planning, using confirmed facts only.
7. Cost guide explaining the variables rather than inventing a price.
8. Enquiry form.
9. About DebsGuru / why choose the team.
10. Privacy, Cookie and Website Terms.

### Phase 2 — organic growth

1. How Much Does a TY Ball Cost in Ireland?
2. Complete TY Ball Planning Checklist.
3. Parent Guide to TY Ball Planning and Safety.
4. Private School TY Ball vs Regional TY Ball.
5. What Can Be Included in a TY Ball Proposal?
6. Original regional pages only when venue, transport and local proof are available.
7. Verified case studies, galleries and independent reviews.

### Separate future product decision

Ticketed regional events, checkout, transfers, refunds, wallet tickets, school dashboards and public event calendars require a separate discovery, specification, budget and legal review. They are not an extension of the current enquiry form.

## Measurement additions

In addition to enquiry conversion:

- landing page to form start;
- form start to accepted enquiry;
- parent/school information engagement;
- cost-guide engagement;
- regional organic landing sessions;
- qualified lead rate by source;
- response time measured internally, without publishing a promise until proven;
- Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile: LCP, INP and CLS.

No school name, contact name, email, phone, free-text message or preferred date is sent to analytics.
