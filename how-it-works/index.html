How it works

From enquiry to event

Start with a date, location and guest estimate.

DebsGuru turns them into a plan the committee can review with confidence.

Share the starting point

Send the school, county, preferred date and a realistic guest estimate. You do not need a final list to begin.

We check what fits

DebsGuru reviews suitable venues and event arrangements for the date, location and expected attendance.

Review one clear proposal

The committee sees the available venue, inclusions, practical requirements and pricing together before deciding.

Confirm the event

The date is secured only after availability, pricing and the booking terms have been agreed with DebsGuru.
