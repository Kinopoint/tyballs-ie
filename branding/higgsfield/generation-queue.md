# Higgsfield generation queue

Use this queue after the Higgsfield MCP tools become available in a newly
opened Codex task. The brand brief in this directory is the source of truth.

## Batch 01 — logo concepts

Create three separate generation jobs. Each job must return a single logo
concept on a flat warm-paper background plus a single-colour navy presentation.
Do not place the logo on stationery, signs, phones, buildings or other mock-ups.

### TYB-LOGO-A — Editorial Rhythm

Design a production-quality identity concept for `TYBalls.ie`, an Irish
Transition Year ball enquiry and planning service operated by DebsGuru Ltd.
Create a confident editorial wordmark with restrained custom serif character,
excellent kerning and a subtle sense of movement. A single purposeful curved
stroke may connect or rhythmically underline TY and Balls, suggesting movement
across a ballroom without depicting a dancer, music note or disco ball. Make
`.ie` fully legible and integrated. Use deep navy #101D32, warm paper #FAF8F3
and one small warm-coral #E9685D accent. Flat vector-like presentation, strong
negative space, crisp edges, suitable for a website header and 16 px favicon.
Show one primary horizontal wordmark and one TY monogram only. Exact spelling:
TYBalls.ie. No tagline, no mock-up, no gradients, no gold, no neon, no confetti,
no shamrock, no crest, no stock-logo symbolism, no extra words.

### TYB-LOGO-B — Invitation Seal

Design a production-quality identity concept for `TYBalls.ie`, an Irish
Transition Year ball enquiry and planning service. Build a compact contemporary
seal from the actual letters T and Y with generous negative space, paired with
an understated, highly legible wordmark. It should recall the care of a modern
printed invitation without looking traditional, aristocratic or ceremonial.
Use deep navy #101D32, warm paper #FAF8F3 and a restrained coral #E9685D accent.
Flat vector-like presentation with one horizontal lockup and one square
monogram. Exact spelling: TYBalls.ie. No tagline, mock-up, wax texture, shield,
crown, laurel, school crest, shamrock, Celtic knot, gold foil, gradients,
nightclub imagery or additional text.

### TYB-LOGO-C — Stage Mark

Design a production-quality identity concept for `TYBalls.ie`, an Irish
Transition Year ball enquiry and planning service. Create a contemporary
geometric wordmark whose spacing and alignment are subtly informed by a
ballroom floor plan or stage grid. Use one distinctive coral interruption to
give the mark rhythm, while keeping it warm and approachable rather than
technical. Use deep navy #101D32, warm paper #FAF8F3 and coral #E9685D. Flat
vector-like presentation, clean construction, one horizontal wordmark and one
square TY symbol. Exact spelling: TYBalls.ie. No tagline, mock-up, spotlights,
disco ball, equaliser, ticket, calendar-check icon, generic event-agency mark,
gradients, chrome, neon or additional words.

## Acceptance check for Batch 01

Each direction is rejected if any of the following is true:

- `TYBalls.ie` is misspelled or punctuation is unclear
- the monogram cannot be reconstructed as simple vector geometry
- the identity relies on gradients, texture or photographic effects
- the favicon becomes unreadable at 16–24 px
- the result resembles a nightclub, school crest or wedding supplier
- a generic icon is placed beside an otherwise unmodified font
- one-colour reproduction loses the defining idea

After review, select one direction and generate only the necessary refinements
for that direction. Do not proceed to photography, icons or motion until the
logo system and final palette are fixed.
