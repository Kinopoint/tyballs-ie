# Selected visual direction — TYB-LOGO-A: Editorial Rhythm

Status: selected on 2026-08-02. This is the active visual source of truth for TYBalls.ie.

## Defining system

- `TYBalls.ie` is always written as one literal string with a clearly legible `.ie`.
- The principal expression is an editorial serif wordmark in deep navy, with a single restrained coral curved stroke that suggests movement without becoming a music, dance or nightlife symbol.
- Coral is a deliberate interruption, never the main field colour or a decorative texture.
- Use warm paper and soft cream as the default surfaces; use deep navy as the decisive contrasting field.
- Composition is calm, spacious and invitation-like. Avoid symmetry-for-ceremony, luxury cues and nightclub energy.

## Required use in every future Higgsfield prompt

Include this direction by name and preserve the approved palette: deep navy `#101D32`, warm paper `#FAF8F3`, soft cream `#F0ECE3`, warm coral `#E9685D`, and brick coral `#BB493F`.

Exclude gradients, metallic effects, neon, confetti, disco imagery, alcohol, school-crests, shamrocks, Celtic stereotypes, fabricated claims and unverified text. Keep critical labels as HTML, not generated into images.
