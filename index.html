Quick answers

Good to know.

Do we need final guest numbers?+

No. Start with a realistic estimate and confirm the final number later.

Who coordinates the event?+

A named DebsGuru coordinator works with the committee, venue and event team.

Can dietary and access needs be planned?+

Yes. Share them early so the selected venue can confirm the arrangements.

Does an enquiry reserve the date?+

No. A date is secured only after availability, pricing and booking terms are agreed.
