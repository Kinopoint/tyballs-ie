Quick answers

Good to know.

Do we need final guest numbers?+

No. A realistic estimate is enough to begin.

Who supports the event on the night?+

A named coordinator works with the venue and responsible adult contacts.

Can dietary and access needs be included?+

Yes. Tell the team early so the venue can confirm arrangements.

Does an enquiry reserve the date?+

No. The date is secured only when the booking terms are agreed.
